Built for people who run multiple projects

Existing window managers tile but forget. Existing workspace tools remember but don't tile. WispTile does both seamlessly.

⚡️

Recall: 1-Shortcut Restore

Switch between projects with ⌥1, ⌥2 or from the menu bar. Reopens exact editors, folders, browser tabs, and positions in under 5 seconds.

📐

Always-On Tiling

Draw custom zones with drag-and-drop. Auto-snap while moving, keyboard shortcuts for halves and quadrants, and customizable window gaps.

🖥️

Multi-Monitor & Notch Safe

Unplug your external display and windows adapt gracefully. Replug it, and they snap back. Strictly respects MacBook Pro notch safe areas.

♻️

Zero Duplicate Windows

If an app is already open, WispTile moves and reuses the existing window. Never clutters your Mac with duplicate browser tabs or editor windows.

🛡️

Active Call Protection

Safety rules detect active meetings in Zoom, FaceTime, and Google Meet—never interrupting or moving your video call during restores.

🔒

Local-First & Private

All projects, layouts, and snapshots stay locally on your Mac. No cloud sync, no accounts required, and zero window contents read.

Free and open source

No price, no account, no license key. WispTile is MIT-licensed and built in the open.
