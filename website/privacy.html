WispTile Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 7, 2026

Summary Guarantee: WispTile is local-first. We do not track what you type, read your screen pixels, inspect document contents, or upload your project files. Your workspace layout is your own.

1. Information Stored Locally on Your Mac

WispTile stores your project configurations, tiling templates, and undo snapshots exclusively on your local Mac disk in ~/Library/Application Support/WispTile/. This includes:

  • Application bundle identifiers and localized app names needed to reopen your workspace.
  • File system paths or URLs that you explicitly choose to include in a project.
  • Window positions and dimensions expressed as relative percentages of your connected displays.
  • Undo snapshots (automatically pruned after 30 days).

2. Information We Never Collect

  • No Screen Recording: WispTile does not capture images, video, or pixels of your screen.
  • No Keystroke Logging: Global shortcuts are registered via standard macOS Carbon/Cocoa event taps solely to listen for assigned trigger combinations.
  • No Document Contents: WispTile never parses or inspects the text inside your editor files or browser tabs.

3. Network Communications

WispTile communicates over the network only for the following explicit reasons:

  • Software Updates: WispTile does not check a server in the background. New versions are published on GitHub Releases.
  • Diagnostics (Explicit User Initiative Only): When clicking "Send Diagnostics to Support" in Settings, you can review all sanitized system information before sending. No diagnostics are ever transmitted automatically without your explicit consent.

4. Data Export & Deletion

You retain full ownership of your data at all times. You can export your entire project and template library to an open JSON format via Settings → Privacy and Data → Export… without paying or activating a license. To completely remove all WispTile data, delete the ~/Library/Application Support/WispTile/ directory.

5. Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue on GitHub.
